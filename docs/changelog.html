Changelog

Newest first. Android versions are listed when they are built; the Play Store line says which one you can install today.

2026-11-10 — Updates, allowances and promotions​

  • Updates reach open tabs by themselves. A small worker that caches nothing notices a new release, and the page loads it at a safe moment. When a release is required, an update screen says why and offers one Update button. See How ZTools stays up to date.
  • Browser tools have no usage limit and need no account. Daily or monthly allowances apply only to the AI assistant, Speak, and tools that process files on our server. Each one is shown beside the feature and depends on your plan. Plans are on the Plans page.
  • One small promotion. The dashboard and tool pages show one clearly marked ad for another product made by the ZTools team, never for ZTools itself. Plans that remove promotions don't show it.
  • New guide: How ZTools stays up to date.

2026-11-07 — Moving to ZTools​

  • Alternatives pages in the app, and Moving to ZTools guides here. Each guide compares one other tool site with dated, sourced facts.

Android 2.31.0 — November 2026 (next Play upload)​

Google Play serves 2.29.0. Version 2.31.0 is built and waiting for upload; it also carries everything listed for 2.30.0, which was never uploaded.

  • Web updates install over the air without a prompt. A new Android build arrives through Play's immediate in-app update.
  • When a newer version is required, the app says so and offers one Update button.
  • The review prompt appears after something worked for you, such as a copied result or a finished tool run, not when the app opens.
  • Bottom sheets stay clear of the navigation bar.

Android 2.30.0 — October 2026 (not uploaded)​

  • A phone layout with an app bar, a bottom bar and sheets.
  • Guided tours of the main pages.
  • A refreshed home and site header.
  • Notifications are turned on from Account settings instead of a prompt on every page.
  • Layout fixes on small screens.

2026-10-28 — Speak guide​

  • New guide: Speak. Record a thought, then review the notes and tasks Speak suggests.

2026-10-27 — Tours, a phone app view and plans​

  • Page tours and a six-screen walkthrough.
  • On a phone, the main screens open as app screens with a bottom bar.
  • A free plan and paid plans, paid through Polar. Read the release post.

2026-10-26 — Sitemap and feed pages​

  • A readable sitemap and feed page, plus /feed.xml. Every sitemap address carries the date it last changed.

2026-07-23 — Full catalog and guides​

  • A page for every tool in the catalog, grouped by category.
  • The first task guides, including the Android app and installing ZTools as an app.

0.1.0 — 2026-05-06​

The docs site opened with Docusaurus 3, pages for 20 of the most used tools, a sitemap and an RSS feed, on ztools-docs.zaions.com through GitHub Pages. Source: github.com/aoneahsan/ztools-docs.
