# Twitter/X Ad Revenue Calculator

Twitter / X ad revenue sharing pays creators a share of ad revenue from impressions on their posts. Eligibility requires Premium subscription + ≥500 followers + ≥5M impressions in 3 months (per X's 2023-2024 program rules). Earnings vary widely — anecdotal data from active creators shows ranges from $0.10 to $5+ per 1000 impressions, heavily dependent on audience verification status, niche, and engagement quality. The ZTools Twitter Ad Revenue Calculator estimates monthly earnings from impressions, follower count, and niche. Important: estimates are illustrative; actual earnings depend on factors X doesn't fully disclose.
