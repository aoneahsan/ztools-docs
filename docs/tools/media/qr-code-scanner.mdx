# qr-code-scanner

A QR code scanner reads QR (Quick Response) codes — square 2D barcodes that encode URLs, plain text, Wi-Fi credentials, vCard contacts, payment instructions, and more — using either a live camera feed or an uploaded image, decoding the embedded data in milliseconds. The ZTools QR Code Scanner runs entirely in the browser using a WebAssembly QR decoder and getUserMedia for live camera access, supports both modes, identifies the data type (URL, text, Wi-Fi, vCard, etc.) and offers context-aware actions like "open URL" or "copy text".

## Use cases

### Quick URL navigation

Posters, packaging, or print ads with QR codes. Scan; visit the URL on your phone or laptop without retyping. Ubiquitous since restaurant menus went QR.

### Wi-Fi credential transfer

Guest at a friend's house. Scan their Wi-Fi QR (generated from network name + password); device joins automatically. No verbal password sharing.

### Contact card import

Conference networking. Scan a vCard QR from a business card; phone offers to add the contact directly to address book.

### Payment / Bitcoin address

Scan a payment QR (Venmo, PayPal, crypto wallet) instead of typing a long address — eliminates typo risk on irreversible transactions.

## How it works

1. **Pick mode** — Camera (live scan) or upload (still image). Camera mode requests webcam permission once.
2. **Camera mode** — Point camera at the QR; the decoder samples frames continuously. As soon as a code is in focus and stable, it decodes — usually within 1-2 seconds.
3. **Upload mode** — Drop or pick an image (JPG / PNG / WebP). Decoder scans the image once. Useful when the QR is on screen elsewhere or in a saved photo.
4. **Identify data type** — Decoded payload classified: URL (http/https), text, Wi-Fi (WIFI:T:WPA;S:…;P:…), vCard (BEGIN:VCARD), email (mailto:), phone (tel:), geo location (geo:).
5. **Take action** — For URLs: open. For text: copy. For Wi-Fi: copy SSID + password. For vCard: download as .vcf for import.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>**What types of QR data does it decode?**</summary>

All standard payloads: plain text, URL, email, phone, SMS, Wi-Fi credentials, vCard contacts, geo coordinates, calendar events, Bitcoin / payment URIs. The decoder reads any standard QR; classification afterward.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Is the camera feed uploaded?**</summary>

No — entirely client-side. Frames are processed by the decoder in browser memory; nothing transmitted to any server.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Why does it sometimes fail to scan?**</summary>

Common causes: QR is too small / blurry / damaged; insufficient lighting; reflective surface; partial occlusion. Move closer, improve light, hold steady.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Can it scan barcodes (EAN / UPC)?**</summary>

No — this tool reads QR codes only. Linear barcodes (1D) require a different decoder. Use the barcode-scanner tool for those.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Are scanned QRs safe to open?**</summary>

Read the URL before opening. Malicious QRs can lead to phishing pages, malware downloads, or auto-payment scams. The tool decodes and shows the destination so you can verify.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Does it work on phones?**</summary>

Yes — webcam permission grants rear-camera access on mobile browsers. Scanning works on iOS Safari, Chrome on Android, etc.

</details>

## Tips

- Hold the camera 4-8 inches from the QR; closer or farther blurs the image.
- Make sure the entire QR is in the frame — partial scans fail.
- Even lighting matters more than bright lighting. Avoid direct flash that creates glare.
- For damaged QRs, try cropping the image and uploading rather than live-camera scan; the decoder has more time to retry.
- Always preview the decoded URL before clicking — QRs are a phishing vector.
