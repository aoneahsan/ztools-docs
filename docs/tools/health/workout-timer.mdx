# workout-timer

A workout timer tracks intervals — work and rest periods — for HIIT, Tabata, circuit training, EMOM (every-minute-on-the-minute), and similar interval-based workouts. The ZTools Workout Timer runs in the browser with audio cues at start, transitions, and finish. Configure work duration, rest duration, number of rounds, prep countdown, and round-of-N. Supports fullscreen for studio / gym use, persists settings across sessions, and works offline once loaded.

## Use cases

### Tabata workout (8 rounds × 20s work + 10s rest)

Classic 4-minute Tabata. Tool counts down each interval with audio cues.

### HIIT (30s work / 30s rest × 10)

10-minute HIIT session. Tool tracks total time + current round.

### Circuit training (5 stations × 45s work + 15s rest, 3 rounds)

Full circuit with multi-round structure.

### EMOM (every-minute-on-the-minute, 20 rounds)

Minute-based intervals. Audio at top of each minute.

## How it works

1. **Configure intervals** — Work duration (1-300s), rest duration (0-300s), rounds (1-50), prep countdown (3-10s).
2. **Pick preset** — Tabata, HIIT, EMOM, circuit, custom.
3. **Start** — Audio "GO!" at start; warning beeps at 3-second countdown to transition; "REST!" at end of work; "GO!" at next round.
4. **Track** — Display: current round, current phase (work/rest), time remaining, total elapsed, total remaining.

## Tips

- For Tabata (30 years of evidence), strict 20/10 × 8 — total 4 min. Don't add extra rest; the discomfort is the training.
- For HIIT, work-to-rest ratio matters: beginners 1:2, intermediate 1:1, advanced 2:1.
- Always include 5-10 min warmup before HIIT — cold starts injure.
- Audio reliability matters mid-workout — keep tab in foreground.
