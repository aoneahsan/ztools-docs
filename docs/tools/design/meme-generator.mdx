# Meme Generator

A meme generator overlays bold caption text — typically white-with-black-stroke Impact font in the classic Internet meme style — onto an uploaded image, producing a shareable PNG ready for social media, group chats, or Slack reactions. The ZTools Meme Generator runs entirely in the browser using HTML5 Canvas, lets you upload your own image or pick from a small bundled template set, supports top and bottom captions plus arbitrary additional text layers, customisable font / size / color / stroke, and exports PNG with no upload to any server.

## Use cases

### Team Slack reactions

Inside-joke meme tied to a specific team event. Generate, post in #random, watch the team react. Memes are office culture's most universally accessible language.

### Social-media engagement

Brand or personal accounts: timely meme on a trending topic outperforms polished marketing for engagement. Speed-of-creation matters more than production quality.

### Educational illustration

Teachers and content creators use memes to make memorable points. Visual humor anchors abstract concepts more effectively than text alone for many learners.

### Custom event / wedding signs

Print a custom-meme-style sign for a party. Generator works for any printable format, not just internet sharing.

## How it works

1. **Upload an image** — JPG / PNG / WebP. Image draws to Canvas at native resolution; aspect ratio preserved.
2. **Add top text** — Default Impact font, white fill, black stroke, all-caps — the classic format. Editable per-text-layer.
3. **Add bottom text** — Same defaults. Optional middle / arbitrary-position text layers for variants.
4. **Customise styling** — Font family, size (responsive to image dimensions), color, stroke width, text alignment.
5. **Export PNG** — Canvas .toBlob() saves to device. Original image untouched in your filesystem.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>**Why Impact font specifically?**</summary>

Cultural convention. The classic Internet meme format from the 2000s used Impact because it was bold and bundled with Windows. The aesthetic stuck. You can change it — but Impact reads as "meme" instantly.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Is the image uploaded?**</summary>

No — Canvas processes entirely in the browser. The image and final meme stay on your device. Privacy by design.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**What about copyright?**</summary>

Memes using copyrighted images may be fair use (parody, commentary), but enforcement varies. For commercial use, prefer images you own or stock from royalty-free sources.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Can I add more than two text layers?**</summary>

Yes — unlimited text layers, each with its own position and styling. Useful for multi-panel formats and dialogue.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Why is my text getting cut off?**</summary>

Auto-shrink fits text to image width. For very long captions, increase image dimensions or break into multiple lines manually.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Are emojis supported?**</summary>

Yes — modern browsers render emoji glyphs in any font. Mix Unicode + plain text freely.

</details>

## Tips

- Keep captions short — under 8-10 words per line. Long captions kill the punch.
- Use the classic Impact-bold-white-stroke format for instantly-readable memes; experiment with other fonts when going for a stylised look.
- Test readability at thumbnail size — memes get viewed at small dimensions on mobile feeds.
- For multi-panel memes (Drake, distracted boyfriend), align text to the visual hot spots in the source image.
- Export at 1080p+ for social media; smaller export hurts on Instagram, fine for chat.
