# Business Card Designer

### Personal contact cards

Non-business contexts: personal contact cards for clubs, hobbies, personal life. Same layout, different content.

## How it works

1. **Pick layout** — Horizontal (3.5×2 in landscape) or vertical. Single-sided or double-sided.
2. **Add front-side content** — Name, title, company, email, phone, website. Logo top-left or center; contact info aligned right or below.
3. **Add back-side content (optional)** — Tagline, QR code, social handles, expanded service list. Less is more — readable beats packed.
4. **Configure for print** — Add 3mm bleed (extends background colors past card edge to prevent white margins after cutting). Use 300 DPI export.
5. **Export PDF / PNG** — PDF preferred for print (vector text, embedded fonts, exact sizing). PNG fine for digital business cards (signed virtual sigs).

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>**What's the standard size?**</summary>

US: 3.5 × 2 inches (89 × 51 mm). Europe: 85 × 55 mm. Japan: 91 × 55 mm. Generator defaults to US; switch in settings.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**What's "bleed"?**</summary>

Extra margin (3mm typical) on all sides where background extends. After cutting, the cut may shift slightly; bleed prevents white edges from showing. Required by all professional printers.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**CMYK vs RGB?**</summary>

Print uses CMYK ink mixing; screens use RGB. Some bright RGB colors don't exist in CMYK and shift on print. For best fidelity, design with print-safe colors or convert before submission.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Where do I print?**</summary>

MOO, VistaPrint, GotPrint for online; local print shops for fast turnaround. Submit your PDF; many will produce 100-250 cards within a week.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**How many should I order?**</summary>

For active networkers: 250-500 first batch. For occasional use: 100. Don't over-order — info changes; designs evolve.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Is anything uploaded?**</summary>

No — Canvas processes entirely in the browser. Your design stays private until you export.

</details>

## Tips

- Keep critical info 5mm inside the card edge — printer cutting tolerance can clip text near the edge.
- Use 1-2 fonts maximum; mixing 3+ fonts looks cluttered.
- White space is a feature, not an absence. Crowded cards look cheap.
- Test print one card before ordering 500 — what looks fine on screen sometimes prints unexpectedly.
- Soft-touch / matte finish + thick stock (350gsm+) signals quality more than design alone. Budget there if you can.
