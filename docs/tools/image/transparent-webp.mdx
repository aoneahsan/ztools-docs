# transparent-webp

Making a WebP transparent replaces a chosen color (typically white or a solid background) with alpha. WebP supports lossless alpha channels — same flexibility as PNG. The ZTools Transparent WebP tool decodes the WebP, lets you eyedropper-pick the colour to remove, applies a tolerance for anti-aliased edges, and outputs a new WebP with the colour swapped to alpha. Original file untouched.

## Use cases

### Remove white background from a logo

Logo on white WebP needs to overlay coloured backgrounds. Convert background to alpha.

### Product photo cutout

Shot on white seamless. Transparent WebP looks better in product grids than visible white square.

### Sticker / cutout for design

Print or web design needs transparent edges. Removing background is the first step.

## How it works

1. **Drop WebP** — Decoded into canvas.
2. **Pick color to remove** — Eyedropper on canvas (typically pick a corner pixel = background) or paste a hex value.
3. **Set tolerance** — How much variation to also remove. Default 15.
4. **Save as WebP** — Output: lossless WebP with alpha.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>**How is WebP transparency different from PNG?**</summary>

Both support lossless alpha. WebP files are typically 25-35% smaller for the same image with alpha.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Tolerance for clean cutout?**</summary>

15-30 catches most anti-aliased edges without eating subject. Below 5 leaves halo; above 50 starts eating subject.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Complex backgrounds?**</summary>

Single-color backgrounds work best. For complex backgrounds, use AI-based background-remover.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Privacy?**</summary>

Browser-only.

</details>

## Tips

- Always pick the background color with eyedropper — typed values often miss by 5-10 RGB points.
- View on a black background to spot remaining halo.
- For complex backgrounds, use a dedicated AI background remover.
- Lossless WebP preserves the alpha exactly — use lossy only for size-critical content.
