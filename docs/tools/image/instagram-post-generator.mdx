## Use cases

### Daily / weekly content series

Tip-of-the-day, quote-of-the-week, or stat-of-the-day series. Templates ensure visual consistency without designer time per post.

### Carousel posts (highest-engagement format)

Carousel posts (up to 10 slides) reach 1.4× more accounts than single-image posts. Generator handles multi-slide layout + consistent branding.

### Quote / testimonial posts

Quote cards from books, customer testimonials, or thought leaders — reliable engagement format requiring just text + good typography.

### Announcement / event posts

Product launch, webinar, hiring, milestone — branded announcement card faster than custom design.

### Brand-aligned reposts

Convert plain copy or external content into your branded format before reposting. Maintains feed cohesion.

## How it works

1. **Pick template category** — Quote, tip, announcement, before/after, listicle, swipeable carousel, statistic, comparison.
2. **Pick post format** — 1080×1080 square (feed), 1080×1350 portrait (feed, more screen), 1080×1920 (Story / Reel), or carousel (10 slides).
3. **Customise content** — Title, body, attribution, accent color, brand logo placement.
4. **Tune visuals** — Font pair (heading + body), background color/gradient/pattern, decorative SVG elements, overlay opacity.
5. **Multi-slide for carousels** — Add slide 2, 3, ..., 10. Each can use a different sub-template; consistent header/footer enforces brand.
6. **Export PNG / ZIP** — Single PNG for posts; ZIP with 10 PNGs for carousels. Direct upload to Instagram or scheduling tool.

## Tips

- Use 1080×1350 portrait — takes more screen space in feed, 25–40% higher engagement on average.
- Carousels reach 1.4× more accounts than single-image posts — use them for evergreen content.
- Pick ONE accent color for your brand and use it across all posts — feed cohesion drives follower retention.
- First slide of a carousel needs a "hook" — explicit "swipe →" cue or curiosity gap.
- Save your template settings (color, font, logo position) — speeds future post creation 5–10×.
