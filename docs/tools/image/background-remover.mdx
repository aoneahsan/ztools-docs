# AI Background Remover

A background remover detects the main subject of a photo and erases everything behind it, producing a transparent PNG cutout that you can drop onto a new background, a product page, or a presentation slide. The ZTools Background Remover runs entirely in your browser using a client-side segmentation model — your image never leaves your device, the result has no watermark, the output is a clean transparent PNG, and you can fine-tune the edge with brush-based add/erase strokes if the auto cut misses a strand of hair or a thin earring.

## Use cases

### E-commerce product photos

Sellers on Etsy, eBay, Amazon, and Shopify need clean white-or-transparent backgrounds to meet listing rules. Shoot the product on any backdrop, run it through the remover, and drop the cutout onto any required background colour in seconds.

### Profile picture cleanup

Replace the cluttered hostel-room background in your LinkedIn headshot with a clean colour or blurred office. Auto-segmentation handles the hair line; brush strokes fix any glasses-frame gaps.

### Marketing graphics and social posts

Designers need subject cutouts for hero banners, Instagram carousels, and pitch decks. Skip the Photoshop pen-tool detour — auto cutouts handle 90% of cases in 5 seconds.

### Passport and ID-style photos

Many countries require a specific solid background for ID photos. Remove the original, paste onto the regulation colour (white, light blue, or red), and export at the required pixel size.

## How it works

1. **Upload your photo** — JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC. Drag-and-drop or paste from clipboard. Up to ~25 MB per file.
2. **Auto-segmentation runs in your browser** — A neural model identifies the foreground subject (person, product, animal, vehicle) and produces an alpha mask in 2–6 seconds depending on device.
3. **Refine the edge if needed** — Switch to the brush tool to add or remove regions — useful for fine hair, glasses, jewellery, or motion blur the auto pass missed.
4. **Pick a new background or keep transparency** — Solid colour, gradient, or another image. Or just export the transparent PNG.
5. **Download** — Transparent PNG (default), JPG with chosen background, or WebP. Original resolution preserved unless you pick a smaller export size.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>**Does my photo upload to a server?**</summary>

No. Segmentation runs in your browser using a client-side model — the file never leaves your device. Faster, private, no quota.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Why is the edge fuzzy on hair?**</summary>

Auto models trade speed for perfect alpha. For portraits with fine flyaway hair, expand the canvas, run the auto pass, then use the soft brush at low opacity to clean the silhouette.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Output is too large — how do I shrink it?**</summary>

Pick a smaller export size before download, or run the result through the Image Compressor. PNG with transparency is naturally larger than JPG; if you do not need transparency, export as JPG with a flat background.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Can it remove the background from a logo?**</summary>

Yes — but for vector logos and screenshots, the magic-wand colour-key tools (Transparent PNG Maker) often give cleaner results because the boundaries are sharp.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Why does the cutout look soft compared to a Photoshop pen path?**</summary>

Auto segmentation produces probability masks, not vector paths. For broadcast-quality cutouts, refine in Photoshop after the auto pass — the auto result is usually 90% there and saves 10–15 minutes per image.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Is there a batch mode?**</summary>

Yes — drop a folder of images and the tool processes them sequentially with the same settings. Ideal for product catalogues with consistent lighting.

</details>

## Tips

- Shoot against a contrasting background when possible — the model finds edges faster and cleaner.
- Hair detail matters? Increase canvas size before running auto so the model has more pixels to work with around the silhouette.
- For e-commerce, after removing background, paste onto pure #FFFFFF and export JPG quality 90 — fastest load, smallest file, most marketplace-compatible.
- Save the transparent PNG as your master and re-composite onto different backgrounds rather than re-cutting each time.
- Use the shadow toggle to preserve a subtle ground shadow — products feel grounded rather than floating.
