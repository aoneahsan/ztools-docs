## How it works

1. **Set author info** — Display name, @handle, profile photo (upload), verified badge toggle.
2. **Write tweet body** — Plain text, supports @mentions, #hashtags, $cashtags with auto-styling. Up to 280 chars (or longer for "X premium" mockups).
3. **Set engagement counts** — Likes, retweets, replies, bookmarks. Realistic 4-digit-and-up formatting (1.2K, 15.3K).
4. **Pick theme** — Light, dark, or dim — matches X's three current display modes.
5. **Set timestamp** — "now", "5m", "2h", date — matches X's relative-time format.
6. **Export PNG** — Pixel-perfect screenshot at 2x DPI for sharp use anywhere.

</details>

## Tips

- Use ethically: clearly label parody/fiction; never fabricate real people's tweets.
- For testimonial mockups, replace with real customer screenshots ASAP after launch — fake testimonials are illegal in many jurisdictions.
- Keep engagement numbers realistic to your scenario (a no-name account with 100K likes screams "fake").
- Match the theme (light/dark) to your destination context — light tweet on dark blog is jarring.
- Export at 2x DPI for retina-quality embedding.
