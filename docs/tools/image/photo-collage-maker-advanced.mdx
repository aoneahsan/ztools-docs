# Photo Collage Maker Advanced

An advanced photo collage maker arranges multiple photos into a single composite image — grid layouts (2×2, 3×3, 4×3), mosaic/asymmetric layouts, freeform drag-and-drop, magazine spreads, polaroid scatter, photo-strip, and themed templates (wedding, travel, family). The ZTools Photo Collage Maker (Advanced) supports up to 30 photos per collage, drag-and-drop reordering, per-photo border / opacity / rotation, captions, background color or pattern, and exports to PNG/JPG at high resolution. Browser-only Canvas — your photos never leave the device.

Print a 24×36" memory board collage for an anniversary gift. Magazine layouts work better than rigid grids.

Compile the year's best photos into a single collage for printing or sharing.

## How it works

1. **Pick a layout** — Grid (2×2 / 3×3 / 4×3), magazine, polaroid scatter, photo-strip, freeform, themed templates.
2. **Add photos** — Drag-drop or upload in batch. Up to 30 per collage.
3. **Per-photo tuning** — Crop, rotate, scale, border, opacity, drop shadow per cell.
4. **Backgrounds** — Solid color, gradient, pattern (paper, grid, wood texture), or photo background.
5. **Captions** — Optional text overlays per photo or for the whole collage. Font/size/color customisable.
6. **Export** — PNG (lossless, supports transparency in scatter/freeform) or JPG (smaller). Up to 4096×4096 for print.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>**How many photos can I include?**</summary>

Up to 30 photos per collage. Performance stays smooth in modern browsers; older devices may slow down with 20+ high-res photos.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**What max output resolution?**</summary>

4096×4096 px for print quality at typical wall-art sizes. Some browsers cap at 8192; fine-tune in advanced settings.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Can I save and resume work?**</summary>

Yes — collages save to browser storage. Reload the page, your in-progress collage is restored.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Will photos be uploaded to a server?**</summary>

No — all processing is local. Photos and the final collage stay in your browser.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Will my collage look the same when printed?**</summary>

Print uses CMYK; screens use RGB. Vivid colors may shift slightly. Order a single test print before committing to large prints.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Best size for IG vs print?**</summary>

IG: 1080×1080 (square) or 1080×1350 (portrait). Print 11×14": 3300×4200 px @ 300 DPI. Print 24×36": 7200×10800 px @ 300 DPI (export as TIFF for max quality).

</details>

## Tips

- Group photos by theme (color, location, time) before laying out — visual cohesion improves dramatically.
- Magazine layouts (one hero + supporting) feel more designed than uniform grids.
- White or off-white borders unify a multi-photo set; matching them sells "this was designed".
- Test print at small size (5×7") before committing to large prints. Color shifts surprise people.
- Save in PNG when transparency / non-rectangular shapes are involved; JPG otherwise.
