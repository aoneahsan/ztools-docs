# Passport Photo Maker

A passport photo maker creates compliant identity photos meeting government specs — exact dimensions (35×45 mm UK, 51×51 mm US, etc.), white/off-white background, head size proportions, neutral expression, no shadows. The ZTools Passport Photo Maker takes a phone selfie, lets you crop to the head/shoulders region with on-screen alignment guides, applies background removal/replacement (white/off-white), and produces both a single passport-size image AND a print-ready 4×6 in / 10×15 cm sheet with 4 or 6 copies for cheap photo-lab printing. Country presets cover US, UK, Schengen, India, Canada, Australia, Japan, and 30+ others.

## How it works

1. **Take or upload photo** — Front-facing selfie, even lighting, neutral expression, eyes open, mouth closed.
2. **Pick country preset** — US, UK, Schengen (EU), India, Canada, Australia, Japan, +30 more. Each loads exact dimensions and head-size requirements.
3. **Auto-crop to head/shoulders** — Face detection aligns the crop; manual fine-tune available.
4. **Background swap** — Auto-detect and replace with white / off-white / light-blue (per country requirement).
5. **Lay out print sheet** — 4×6 in or 10×15 cm sheet with 4 or 6 copies arranged for kiosk printing.
6. **Export** — High-resolution JPG (300 DPI) for printing or upload to government portal.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>**Will it pass government inspection?**</summary>

Compliance depends on lighting / pose / expression — the tool handles dimensions, background, and crop. If the source photo has shadows / wrong expression / closed eyes, no tool fixes that. Take care with the original.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**What lighting do I need?**</summary>

Even soft light, no harsh shadows. Stand 2 feet from a window with curtains drawn (diffused daylight). Avoid direct sun, ceiling spotlights, or single-source lamps.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Is the background removal AI?**</summary>

Yes — uses an in-browser ML model (≈10 MB) for foreground/background segmentation. No upload, runs locally.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Can I print at home?**</summary>

Yes if your printer does 300 DPI on photo paper. Otherwise, email to a photo lab or take to a kiosk (CVS, Walgreens, FedEx) — print 4×6 sheets at $0.10–0.30 each.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Why does my head look the wrong size?**</summary>

Each country has strict head-height-to-frame ratio. Use the on-screen overlay guide; if your photo doesn't fit, retake from closer or farther distance.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Are smiles allowed?**</summary>

Most countries require neutral expression with mouth closed. Some allow slight smiles (US, UK lenient; Schengen strict). Check the country preset's notes.

</details>

## Tips

- Even diffused daylight from a window gives the best result; avoid overhead spotlights.
- Wear a contrasting color shirt (don't wear white if background is white).
- Eyes open, mouth closed, neutral expression, looking straight at the camera.
- Print on glossy photo paper; matte paper looks "wrong" against IDs and passports.
- Check your country's exact spec — some require eyeglasses off, no head coverings, full ear visibility.
