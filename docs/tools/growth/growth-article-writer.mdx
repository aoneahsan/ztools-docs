# AI Article Writer

Give AI Article Writer a topic, optional target keywords, and a tone. It makes three requests: outline, draft, then polish. There's no editing pause between them. While signed in, you can choose ZTools' shared AI route or use a provider key you connect. Shared requests use the daily AI allowance; your provider may bill own-key requests. Your monthly article limit also applies. Read and fact-check the draft before publishing. The tool doesn't verify claims or guarantee search placement or AI citations.

## Use cases

### Start a topic draft

Enter a topic, optional target keywords, and a tone. The tool builds an outline, draft, and polish pass without asking you to approve the outline between steps.

### Compare tones

Run separate articles with different tone choices when you need to compare how the first draft reads. Each run uses the monthly article limit and either shared allowance or provider requests.

### Prepare for editing

Copy the polished output into your editor. Check claims, add primary sources, test any code, and decide what to publish.

## How it works

1. **Choose an AI route** — Choose shared AI while signed in or a provider you've connected. The tool then asks for an outline, a draft, and a polish pass; each shared request uses the daily AI allowance.
2. **Enter the topic** — Enter a topic, optional target keywords, and one of the available tone choices. There is no field for an audience, source document, or arbitrary brief.
3. **Generate the outline and draft** — The tool generates an outline and immediately passes it into the drafting request. You cannot edit the outline between those calls.
4. **Polish the draft** — The third request polishes the draft for wording and structure. Read it as a first draft that still needs human editing.
5. **Review and fact-check** — Copy the final text and check its claims, quotes, product details, and any suggested meta description before publishing.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>**Is the AI Article Writer free?**</summary>

The Free plan has a monthly article limit, and paid Growth plans may raise it. One article makes three AI requests. With shared AI, those requests use your daily allowance; with your own key, the provider may bill your account. Check the current limits before starting.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Do I need my own API key, and where is usage billed?**</summary>

No own key is required if you're signed in and the shared AI option has allowance available. You can instead connect a supported provider key. Own-key generation is billed under that provider's terms.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Will the articles be accurate and ready to publish?**</summary>

No. Treat the output as a first draft. Check facts, quotes, product details, and code against current sources before publishing.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Why do my results vary so much?**</summary>

Results depend on the topic, keywords, tone, and AI route you choose. The tool runs a fixed three-pass chain; it does not verify the facts in its output.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**What does a multi-step prompt chain mean here?**</summary>

It makes an outline request, passes that outline directly into a draft request, then polishes the draft. The current screen doesn't pause for outline approval.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Can I control the model and writing voice?**</summary>

You choose one of the screen's tone options and an available AI route. The shared route's model is chosen by FilesHub, not by a model selector in ZTools.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Does it optimize the article for search and AI answer engines?**</summary>

The tool asks for headings and a suggested meta description. It cannot guarantee rankings or AI citations. Add original reporting and verify every claim before publishing.

</details>

## Tips

- Use a specific topic and target keywords; the current screen has no separate brief or source-text field.
- If you use your own key, review that provider's charges; the shared route uses ZTools' daily allowance.
- Keep a fact-check pass: verify every statistic, quote, and product claim against a primary source.
- Check your current monthly article limit and daily AI allowance before a three-call run.
