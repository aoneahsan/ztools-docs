# Analytics Chat

Connect a Google Analytics 4 property and pull its current default report for the last 28 days, or paste analytics data into the chat. Ask about the data you supplied. ZTools sends attached or pasted data to your chosen AI route for an explanation. Your question doesn't build a new GA4 query. Shared AI uses the daily allowance, and your own key may incur provider charges. Your plan's monthly Analytics Chat limit also applies. Check figures against their source before acting.

Analytics Chat is part of Growth Suite. Sign in to see its current allowance and any connection or provider key it needs. Limits vary by plan; a limited Free plan is available.
:::

## Use cases

### Ask about a pulled GA4 report

Connect a GA4 property, pull its current default report for the last 28 days, and ask a question about those rows. Confirm any figure in GA4.

### Ask about pasted data

Paste a GA4 or Search Console excerpt into the chat when you don't want to connect a property. The AI explains only what you supplied.

### Prepare a status note

Copy a useful explanation into your own note after checking it against the source report. The tool doesn't build a new GA4 query from your wording.

## How it works

1. **Connect GA4 or prepare data** — Optionally connect a Google account with access to a GA4 property. You can also paste analytics data without that connection.
2. **Choose an AI route** — Choose shared AI while signed in, or use a provider key you've connected. Shared requests use the daily allowance; own-key calls may be billed by your provider.
3. **Supply data and ask** — Pull the default last-28-days GA4 report or paste data, then ask a question about it.
4. **Read the fixed report** — The pull uses the app's fixed GA4 report; your question does not translate into a new GA4 Data API query.
5. **Verify the explanation** — Read the AI answer and verify its figures in GA4 or in the pasted source before sharing it.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>**Is Analytics Chat free, and what does it cost?**</summary>

Analytics Chat is available within the Free plan's monthly question limit. Paid Growth plans may raise it. A shared AI answer uses the daily allowance; a request through your own provider key may create a separate provider charge.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**How accurate are the answers?**</summary>

The answer is an AI reading of the report you pulled or data you pasted. It may misread a label or number; verify important figures in the source.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Which keys or connections do I need?**</summary>

To pull live GA4 data, connect a Google account with access to the property. You can also paste analytics data without connecting GA4. For the explanation, choose shared AI if your account has allowance, or connect your own AI provider key. Attached or pasted data goes to the selected AI provider. For shared AI, ZTools sends it from its server; FilesHub supplies connection credentials.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Can it change my analytics property?**</summary>

No. The chat reads data you pull or paste and writes an explanation; it does not edit your GA4 property.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**What data sources does it support?**</summary>

You can pull the app's default GA4 report for a connected property. You may also paste analytics data, including Search Console rows. The chat does not connect directly to other data sources or build new GA4 queries from your question.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**How can I get a useful answer?**</summary>

Attach or paste the exact data you want read, ask one focused question, and verify the reply against that source.

</details>

## Tips

- State which attached or pasted rows you mean and the comparison you want.
- Use the metric names in your source table so the AI can follow them.
- Ask one question at a time and check the answer against the supplied data.
- Before sharing a figure, confirm it in the source report.
- Check the daily AI allowance for shared requests or your provider dashboard for own-key charges.
