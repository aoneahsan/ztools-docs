## Use cases

### Plan a topic cluster

Enter a seed term and review the phrases Google Suggest returns. Use a relevant question as a starting point for a page, then verify demand separately.

### Find customer wording

Run a product or service term and compare the returned modifiers with your own customer language. The tool does not rank them by search volume.

### Prepare a content brief

Copy relevant phrases into a brief, then check demand and competition in a separate data source before committing a budget.

## How it works

1. **Enter a seed keyword** — Type a seed term. The current screen has no language or region control.
2. **Query Google Suggest** — The tool sends the seed through ZTools' URL proxy to Google Suggest and reads any returned completions.
3. **Expand variations** — The tool queries Google Suggest for the seed and seven prefixed variations: how to, what is, why is, when to, where to, best, and vs. It does not expand a–z.
4. **Group returned phrases** — It deduplicates the returned phrases and labels them direct, question, or long-tail. It does not score difficulty.
5. **Review and copy** — Review the phrases and copy the ones you want. Export is a copied text list, not a volume report.

## Tips

- Start with a seed that matches the subject you plan to cover.
- Review the question group for phrases that match questions your readers actually ask.
- Use a comparison suggestion only when it fits your subject; the tool does not measure commercial intent.
- Validate search volume and competition elsewhere before budgeting work.
- Copy returned phrases into your own list and remove duplicates across separate searches.
