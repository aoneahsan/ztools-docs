# Page Speed Scan

Enter a public URL and choose mobile or desktop. From your browser, Page Speed Scan calls Google's PageSpeed Insights API and organizes the Lighthouse results into a performance score, metrics, opportunities, and diagnostics. You don't need a personal Google API key for the normal scan. Your plan's monthly scan limit applies, and Google may rate-limit requests. The displayed metrics are lab results for that run. They don't guarantee what every visitor experiences.

## Use cases

### Check a public preview

Scan a publicly reachable preview page on mobile, then scan it again after a change. Compare the Lighthouse results without treating one score as a launch guarantee.

### Inspect mobile opportunities

Run a mobile scan of a public page and review the returned opportunities. This screen does not show CrUX field data or diagnose a visitor's real INP.

### Track a change yourself

After a site change, run another scan and record its score alongside the earlier one in your own notes. ZTools does not save a scan history.

## How it works

1. **Paste the public URL to audit** — Enter the full public URL. The page must be accessible to Google's servers for the scan to run.
2. **Choose a strategy** — Choose mobile or desktop for one scan. Run again with the other strategy if you want both.
3. **Run PageSpeed Insights** — Your browser sends the URL to Google's PageSpeed Insights API. Google performs the Lighthouse run; no personal Google key is required.
4. **Review Lighthouse results** — The screen shows the Lighthouse performance score, audit metrics, opportunities, and diagnostics. It does not show a separate CrUX field-data view.
5. **Review suggested work** — Review the returned opportunities and diagnostics. Their ordering does not prove how much a change will improve your score.
6. **Apply a change and scan again** — Apply a change on your site and run another lab scan. Keep your own record if you want to compare earlier results.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>**Is Page Speed Scan free, or what does it cost?**</summary>

The Free plan includes Page Speed scans up to its monthly limit. Paid Growth plans may raise the limit. There is no ZTools per-scan checkout; check the current plan comparison for your allowance.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Do I need a Google API key or any key of my own?**</summary>

You don't need to add your own Google API key. The scan sends the URL to Google's PageSpeed Insights API from your browser. Google may limit requests or decline a page it can't reach.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Are these scores what my real users experience?**</summary>

The score and metrics shown here come from a Lighthouse lab run. They are not direct measurements of every visitor's experience. Check a field-data source separately if you need real-user measurements.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**If I apply every recommended fix, will my score jump to 100?**</summary>

No. The returned opportunities and diagnostics are suggestions, not a proven priority order or a promise of a particular score change. Run another scan after a change to see what the next lab test reports.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**What is the difference between desktop and mobile scans?**</summary>

Each scan chooses mobile or desktop. Run each separately to compare results; they may differ because Lighthouse uses different test settings.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Can I scan a staging or password-protected page?**</summary>

The scan runs through Google's servers, so it needs a publicly reachable page. A private page may fail; use a public preview when appropriate.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Why does field (CrUX) data sometimes not appear?**</summary>

This screen reports Lighthouse metrics returned by the scan. It doesn't currently display a separate CrUX field-data panel, even if Google's API response contains field data. Use PageSpeed Insights directly if you need that comparison.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Does Page Speed Scan keep my pages or send my data anywhere?**</summary>

Your browser sends the URL you enter to Google's PageSpeed Insights API. ZTools checks your monthly scan allowance. The current Page Speed screen doesn't show a saved scan history.

</details>

## Tips

- Run a mobile scan when the phone layout matters to your task.
- Run another scan after a change to see what the next lab test reports.
- Review the returned opportunities, then measure again; the list does not guarantee a score gain.
- Use a publicly reachable preview URL if Google can access it.
- Keep your own dated record if you want to compare Lighthouse metrics across releases.
