# GA4 Mastery

Connect a Google Analytics 4 property and choose one of three preset reports: top pages, acquisition by channel, or device and browser activity. Each uses a fixed definition for the last 28 days. ZTools fetches the data and asks your chosen AI route to explain it. Shared AI uses the daily allowance; your provider may charge for own-key requests. Your plan's monthly GA4 Mastery limit also applies. Verify figures and conclusions in GA4.

:::info Growth Suite
GA4 Mastery is part of Growth Suite. Sign in to see its current allowance and any connection or provider key it needs. Limits vary by plan; a limited Free plan is available.
:::

## Use cases

### Review top pages

Connect a GA4 property and run the top-pages report for the fixed recent window. Check any page-level conclusion against GA4.

### Review acquisition

Run the acquisition-by-channel report to read the returned sessions, new users, and engagement rates. The AI's explanation is a starting point.

### Review devices and browsers

Run the device-and-browser report to compare the returned groups, then verify a surprising result in GA4.

## How it works

1. **Connect GA4** — Connect a Google account with access to the GA4 property you want to read.
2. **Choose an AI route** — Choose shared AI while signed in or an AI provider key you've connected. Shared requests use the daily allowance; own-key calls may be billed by your provider.
3. **Pick a preset** — Choose top pages, acquisition by channel, or device and browser. Those are the three current presets.
4. **Use the fixed window** — Each preset uses a fixed recent reporting window; the current screen has no custom date-range control.
5. **Generate the report** — The selected route receives the returned GA4 rows and writes an explanation you can read or copy.
6. **Check before acting** — Verify the rows and conclusions in GA4 before using the explanation in a decision.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>**Is GA4 Mastery free, and what does it cost?**</summary>

<details>
<summary>**Do I need my own API keys?**</summary>

Connect a Google account with access to the GA4 property. To generate the written report, use the available shared AI route or connect your own AI provider key. The relevant GA4 data is sent to the selected route.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**How accurate are the reports?**</summary>

An AI report can misread the GA4 rows or imply a cause that the rows do not establish. Verify figures and conclusions in GA4.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Can I write my own custom queries or SQL?**</summary>

No. The current screen offers three presets: top pages, acquisition by channel, and device and browser. It has no arbitrary SQL, funnel, retention, or custom-date analysis.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**What data does it access, and where does it go?**</summary>

The tool reads reporting data from the GA4 property you connect. For a shared report, ZTools sends relevant metrics directly from its server to the selected AI provider, using credentials from FilesHub; with your own key, they go to that provider. The tool doesn't edit your GA4 property. Review sensitive data before running an analysis.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Does the quality of insight vary?**</summary>

The AI explanation depends on the data returned and the selected route. Missing or sparse GA4 data can make an answer less useful; check the raw rows.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Which analyses are included?**</summary>

The current presets are top pages, acquisition by channel, and device and browser, each using a fixed recent window.

</details>

## Tips

- Choose the preset that matches the question you can answer with top pages, acquisition, or device and browser rows.
- Check that GA4 is collecting the events and page data you expect before reading the report.
- Treat an AI explanation as a hypothesis and check the returned figures in GA4.
- Copy the result into your own record if you need a history; the screen does not save a report series.
- With your own key, review provider charges; with shared AI, check the daily allowance.
