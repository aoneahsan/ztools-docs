# Bill of Sale Generator

A bill of sale generator is a tool that fills a general bill-of-sale template from a few details — the seller and buyer, a description of the item, the sale price, and the date — to produce a ready-to-print, fill-in document that records a transfer of ownership. A bill of sale is the paper trail proving that one party sold and another bought a specific item for an agreed amount on a given day. This generator lets you choose whether the item is sold "as-is," with no guarantees, or with a seller’s warranty of clear title, and lays out the finished text for signing. Everything is assembled in your browser. It is a general template, not legal advice.

## Use cases

### Document a used-car sale between private parties

When you sell a car privately, both sides usually want written proof of the price, the date, and that ownership changed hands. Filling in the parties, the vehicle description, and the amount produces a clean record each person can keep. Note that many places also require a separate title transfer and odometer disclosure, which this general template does not replace.

### Record a boat, trailer, or equipment sale

Boats, trailers, motorcycles, and business or farm equipment are often sold person to person for meaningful sums. A bill of sale captures what was sold, for how much, and to whom, giving both parties a reference if a question arises later. For titled items like boats or trailers, complete any required registration or title paperwork separately.

### Sell personal property as-is

For furniture, electronics, tools, collectibles, or other secondhand goods, an as-is bill of sale makes clear that the buyer accepts the item in its current condition with no warranty. Spelling that out up front reduces the chance of a later dispute over faults discovered after the sale, and gives the seller a signed acknowledgement that the terms were understood.

### Provide a receipt with a title warranty

When you want to assure a buyer that you genuinely own the item and it is free of liens, choosing the warranty-of-clear-title option adds language to that effect. This can give a buyer more confidence in a higher-value purchase, though it also means you are standing behind that promise — so only select it if it is actually true.

### Create a dated record for accounting or resale

Both sides can benefit from a dated record of a private sale for personal accounting, insurance, or a future resale. Because a bill of sale notes the amount and the date, it can help substantiate what was paid, though whether sales tax applies is determined by your local rules, not by the document itself.

## How it works

1. **Enter the seller and buyer details** — Type each party’s full legal name and address so the document clearly identifies who is transferring and who is receiving ownership.
2. **Describe the item** — Add a specific description — for a vehicle include make, model, year, and VIN; for other goods, enough detail to identify exactly what is being sold.
3. **Set the price and date** — Enter the agreed sale amount and the date of sale, which together define the core terms of the transfer.
4. **Choose as-is or with warranty** — Select whether the item is sold as-is with no guarantees, or with a seller’s warranty of clear title, and the template inserts the matching clause.
5. **Print, review, and sign** — Generate the finished document, read it carefully, then print it for both parties to sign and date — adding any locally required paperwork separately.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>**What is a bill of sale?**</summary>

It is a written record documenting that a seller transferred ownership of a specific item to a buyer for an agreed price on a certain date. It serves as proof of the transaction for both parties and, for some items, as part of the paperwork needed to register or title the property.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Is a bill of sale legally binding?**</summary>

Once both parties sign it, a bill of sale generally serves as evidence of the agreement and the transfer. However, legal requirements vary by place and item, and some transfers need additional documents to be complete. This tool produces a general template, not a guarantee that it meets your jurisdiction’s specific rules.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**What does "as-is" mean?**</summary>

Selling as-is means the buyer accepts the item in its present condition, with no warranty about its quality or that it is free of defects. It shifts the risk of hidden faults to the buyer, which is why it is common for used goods sold between private parties.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Do I need to notarize it?**</summary>

It depends on your location and the item. Some states or transactions require notarization, an odometer disclosure for vehicles, or witnesses; others do not. Check your local rules — this template does not determine or fulfill those requirements for you.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Does this handle title transfer or sales tax?**</summary>

No. A bill of sale records the sale, but transferring a title, registering a vehicle or boat, and paying any sales or use tax are separate steps handled through your local motor-vehicle or tax authority.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Is my information uploaded anywhere?**</summary>

No. The document is assembled entirely in your browser. The names, prices, and details you enter are not sent to a server or stored by the tool.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Is this legal advice?**</summary>

No. This is a general, editable template offered for convenience. Requirements differ by state and by the type of item, so for anything important or high-value, have an attorney review the document or advise you.

</details>

## Tips

- Describe the item precisely — for vehicles include the VIN, make, model, and year so there is no ambiguity about what was sold.
- Give each party a signed copy and keep yours with your records.
- Check whether your state requires notarization, an odometer statement, or a witness before you rely on the document.
- Only choose the warranty-of-clear-title option if you truly own the item free of any liens.
- Remember a bill of sale does not transfer a title or register a vehicle — complete those steps separately.
