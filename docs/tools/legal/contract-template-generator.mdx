# contract-template-generator

A contract template is a pre-written legal agreement with fillable fields — for NDAs, freelance services, employment, partnerships, sales. Templates speed up routine contracts and ensure standard clauses (governing law, dispute resolution, termination) aren't accidentally omitted. The ZTools Contract Template Generator offers 10+ templates with field-by-field customization, multi-jurisdiction language hints (US / UK / EU), and PDF export. Important: templates are starting points, not legal advice. Always review with a lawyer for important contracts.

For early-stage co-founders before formal incorporation.

## Tips

- For ANY contract over a few thousand dollars or with ongoing obligations, have a lawyer review.
- Always include governing-law and dispute-resolution clauses — without them, jurisdictional fights become expensive.
- For NDAs, mutual is usually fairer than one-way — easier to get the other side to sign.
- Save signed copies in multiple places (email + filesystem + cloud) — disputes years later need original docs.
