# Return & Refund Policy Generator

A return / refund policy is a legal document detailing how customers can return purchases and receive refunds. Required by most e-commerce platforms (Shopify, WooCommerce, Stripe), expected by customers, and legally regulated in many jurisdictions (EU 14-day cooling-off period, UK Consumer Rights Act, US state-by-state). The ZTools Return / Refund Policy Generator builds a customizable policy with standard clauses (return window, condition requirements, exceptions, refund method, shipping responsibility), with jurisdiction-specific language hints. Important: not legal advice — review with a lawyer before publishing.

## Tips

- For EU sales, 14-day cooling-off is non-negotiable. Anything stricter is illegal there.
- For UK, the Consumer Rights Act 30-day short-term right to reject is statutory.
- For US, return policies vary by state — California requires conspicuous disclosure of certain restrictions.
- Always disclose restocking fees / shipping responsibility upfront. Hidden costs trigger consumer-protection violations.
