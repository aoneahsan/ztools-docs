## Use cases

### Cooking with international recipes

A British recipe calls for "200 g flour and 150 ml milk". Convert to US cups for the kitchen. Avoids the "approximate it and hope" approach.

### Travel and shopping abroad

Hotel says room is "32 m²" — that's 344 sq ft. A 5 km run is 3.1 miles. Quick conversions while on the road.

### Engineering and science homework

Convert 4.5 kJ to calories, 60 mph to m/s, 1 atm to kPa. The converter handles the unit math; you focus on the physics.

### Construction and DIY measurements

Lumber sold in feet, plans in metric. Convert quickly without misreading the tape and cutting an inch short.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>**Why are US and UK gallons different?**</summary>

1 US gallon = 3.785 L; 1 UK (imperial) gallon = 4.546 L. Different historical definitions. Always check which "gallon" a recipe or spec means — about 20% difference.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Is the conversion exact?**</summary>

For most units, yes — modern SI definitions are exact. Inch is exactly 25.4 mm. Pound is exactly 0.45359237 kg. Some derived units (BTU, calorie) have multiple definitions; the converter notes which it uses.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**How do I convert temperature?**</summary>

Temperature isn't a simple multiplication — it has an offset. °F to °C: (F − 32) × 5/9. °C to K: C + 273.15. The converter handles all the offsets correctly; don't try mental math for temperature.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Can it convert currency?**</summary>

No — currency conversion needs live exchange rates. Use the dedicated Currency Converter for that. This tool covers physical units only.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**What's the difference between mass and weight?**</summary>

Mass is the amount of matter (kg, lb-mass). Weight is force (N, lb-force) and depends on gravity. Outside physics class, "pounds" and "kilograms" are usually used as mass; the converter follows that convention.

</details>

## Tips

- For very large or very small values, use scientific notation (1e6 = 1,000,000) — the converter handles it.
- Keep a few common conversions memorized: 1 mile ≈ 1.6 km, 1 inch = 2.54 cm, 1 kg ≈ 2.2 lb, 0 °C = 32 °F.
- For cooking, weight is more accurate than volume — 200 g of flour is consistent; 1 cup varies with packing.
- When precision matters (engineering, medicine), always work in SI units and convert at the end.
