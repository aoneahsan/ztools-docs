## Use cases

### Instagram caption hashtags

A photographer posts a sunset shot. Topic "sunset photography". Generate a balanced 30-tag mix: 5 mass (#sunset), 15 mid (#sunsetphotography), 10 niche (#goldenhourshots).

### TikTok video tags

TikTok thrives on a small set of trending tags. Generate 4–6 with at least one trending tag plus topic-specific ones.

### LinkedIn professional posts

B2B post about productivity. LinkedIn rewards 3–5 well-chosen hashtags. Generator suggests #productivity, #remotework, #futureofwork.

### X / Twitter campaign tags

A campaign needs 1–2 tags. Generator surfaces the dominant tag in your niche plus a campaign-specific one.

## How it works

1. **Enter your topic** — A keyword phrase ("sunset photography", "AI productivity", "indie game dev"). Or paste your post caption — the generator extracts topic words.
2. **Pick the platform** — Instagram (30 tags ideal), TikTok (3–6), X (1–2), LinkedIn (3–5), YouTube (10–15 in description), Pinterest (8–10).
3. **Choose tier mix** — Mass (10K+ posts), mid (1K–100K), niche (&lt; 10K). Default mix is platform-specific — Instagram weights niche heavily.
4. **Filter exclusions** — Blocked / shadowbanned list filtered out. Custom exclusions accepted (block competitor brand tags, NSFW tags, etc.).
5. **Copy formatted** — Instagram prefers tags on their own line below the caption. X inline. LinkedIn at the end of the post.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>**How many hashtags should I use on Instagram?**</summary>

Instagram allows up to 30 in a caption. Recent guidance from Meta itself recommends 3–5 for reach optimization, but creators in many niches still see better results with the full 30. Test both.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Are mass hashtags useless?**</summary>

Not useless — they bring brief impressions. But mid- and niche-tier hashtags drive higher engagement-per-impression because the audience self-selected. Mix all three tiers.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**How do I know if a tag is shadowbanned?**</summary>

The generator maintains an updated exclusion list. Manually verify by searching the tag in-app — if recent posts show no results, treat it as banned.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Can I mix LinkedIn and Instagram tags?**</summary>

No — different platforms reward different tag styles. LinkedIn prefers single broad tags; Instagram a long mix. Generate separately per platform.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Why does the generator suggest 30 tags for Instagram?**</summary>

It is the platform maximum, and most niche creators still see better reach by saturating. The tier mix is what matters; the count is a side effect.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Is the data uploaded?**</summary>

No — keyword expansion runs against a local dictionary; nothing about your post leaves the browser.

</details>

## Tips

- Mix tiers: 1 mass, 1 mid, 1 niche per 3 hashtags is a safe starting ratio.
- Save your topic's best tag mix as a preset — re-running the generator on every post is unnecessary.
- Avoid the same 30 tags on every post — Instagram's recent guidance discourages "tag stuffing"; vary the niche third per post.
- On LinkedIn, place hashtags at the end of the post separated by spaces — they are clickable and indexed.
- On X, less is more — 1–2 well-chosen tags beat 5 noisy ones.
