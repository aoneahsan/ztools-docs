# Updates and renewals

Every licence and buyout includes six months of updates from the purchase date.

## What an update is

- New tools and fixes to existing ones, released on ZTools first.
- Security fixes and dependency upgrades.
- New releases of the platforms your licence covers, built with your name.

## When the included months end

Your deployment keeps running on its last version. Nothing switches off.

To start updates again, renew for 3, 6 or 12 months on the [updates page](https://ztools.zaions.com/white-label/updates) with your licence key and the email the licence was issued to. A renewal adds its months to the later of today and your current end date, so renewing early loses nothing.

The renewal prices are on that page, read from our licensing service.
