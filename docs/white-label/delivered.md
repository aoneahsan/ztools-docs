# What each platform delivers

A licence covers the platform groups you pay for. You can take one, two or all three; [the pricing page](https://ztools.zaions.com/white-label/packages) shows the total for any mix.

## Web app

The full ZTools web app on your domain: every tool, the dashboard, sign-in, plans and the docs links, under your name. We connect the domain, serve it over HTTPS and deploy each release.

## Android and iOS app

The Android app, built with your name and icon and published under your Google Play developer account. The store account is yours, because the listing carries your name.

iOS is part of the same group. It's delivered once you provide or fund an Apple developer account.

## Browser extension

The extension for Chrome, Firefox and Edge, with your name and icon, listed under your store accounts.

## What every platform shares

- Your name, logo and colours, applied by us.
- Six months of updates from the purchase date, then renewals if you want more.
- The licence key and the public licence file described in [Check a licence](/docs/white-label/verify).

## What no platform includes

- Source code. That only comes with a buyout.
- Changes made only for you. Those are [custom work](https://ztools.zaions.com/white-label/custom-work), quoted on their own.
- Your domain, store accounts and paid services you choose to add.
- Usage costs for shared AI and the tools that run on our processing server. Which of those your deployment includes is agreed before setup.
