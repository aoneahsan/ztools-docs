# White-label questions

## Do I get the source code with a licence?

No. We host and run ZTools for you. The code changes hands only with a [buyout](https://ztools.zaions.com/white-label/buyout).

## Can you license ZTools to my competitor?

Yes, until someone buys it outright. After a buyout we stop selling it, and licences sold before the sale keep their terms.

## Can I have only some of the tools, or my own changes?

A licence covers the whole workbench. A smaller set for one audience, a tool only your users need, or your own sign-in is [custom work](https://ztools.zaions.com/white-label/custom-work), quoted separately.

## How do I pay?

By Wise bank transfer, for the exact total the pricing page shows. You send the transfer reference on our payment page; once we confirm the payment, the licence key and certificate arrive by email.

## Is iOS included in the mobile group?

Yes. It's delivered once you provide or fund an Apple developer account.

## What does a buyout hand over?

The source code of every repository that builds the web app, the Android app and the browser extension, with six months of updates. Hosting, domains, store accounts and paid services then become yours to run.

## Is there anything to pay after a buyout?

Yes, a royalty. The buyer pays us 7% of the gross revenue ZTools earns, not counting sales tax or VAT they collect. It's paid every quarter with a revenue statement, for as long as ZTools earns revenue, and it's still owed if they sell ZTools on to someone else.

## Where are the full terms?

The binding text is the "White-label licences and buyouts" section of the [Terms of Service](https://ztools.zaions.com/terms#white-label). The [plain-words version](https://ztools.zaions.com/white-label/license-terms) says the same things more simply.
