# From payment to launch

1. **Pay by Wise transfer.** The payment page shows the total the pricing page showed and our Wise details. Send the transfer, then give us its reference there; we confirm it and email your licence key.
2. **Read the licence email.** It arrives once we confirm the transfer and holds your licence key and certificate. The thank-you page after you submit the reference only confirms the order; the email is the record.
3. **Reply with your details.** Send the domain you'll run it on, your logo (SVG if you have one), your colours and the name to show.
4. **Point the domain.** We send the DNS record to add. Until it resolves, the deployment waits.
5. **We build and deploy.** Your name and colours go into every page, tool and email. For Android, we prepare the build for your Play listing.
6. **We tell you it's live.** Your licence then shows as active on the [verify page](https://ztools.zaions.com/verify-license).

How long each step takes depends mostly on DNS and, for Android, on the store's review. We say where things stand at each step.
