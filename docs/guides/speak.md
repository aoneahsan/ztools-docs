# Speak

Record a thought. Then review any notes or tasks Speak suggests.

## Capture first. Choose what stays.

Recording and playback do not use a Speak extraction. You can record up to 15 minutes.

Record a note from any screen.

## Review what Speak suggests

Saved recordings appear here. Review one to choose which notes and tasks to keep.

Speak suggests. You review. Your tools receive only the items you choose to save.

## Meeting notes

Record up to 15 minutes. Speak suggests a summary, action items, and decisions, and you choose what goes into the form.

## Where recordings go

When you save a Speak recording, ZTools stores its audio privately on FilesHub and sends the audio to Groq for a server transcript.

ZTools keeps a saved Speak or meeting recording until you delete it or your account is deleted.

Read the [Privacy Policy](https://ztools.zaions.com/privacy-policy) for every service a recording reaches.
