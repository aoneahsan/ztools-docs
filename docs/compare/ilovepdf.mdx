# Moving from iLovePDF to ZTools

iLovePDF is a PDF tool site with web, mobile and desktop apps and a paid Premium plan. If PDFs are your whole day and you want the same tools on web, phone and desktop, with signatures and workflows, it is built for you.

ZTools makes more sense when PDFs are one job among many: text, image, code and data tools sit in the same place, and most run in your browser. There is no account or file import, so moving means opening your next job here instead.

**Facts last checked Nov 7, 2026.** Prices change, so check them on <a href="https://www.ilovepdf.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">iLovePDF's site</a>.

## Switching over

1. Download anything you keep in iLovePDF first. Nothing carries across.
2. Take a file that does not matter through your most common job on the matching page above, and read the result.
3. Before you sign anything real with Sign PDF, open the signed copy and check it looks the way you expect.
4. Used the iLovePDF phone app? The ZTools [Android app](/docs/guides/android-app) is on Google Play, and the browser [extension](/docs/guides/browser-extension) puts the tools one click away on a computer.

## Where iLovePDF stays ahead

- A desktop app. ZTools runs on the web, on Android and as a browser extension.
- Workflows and team seats, built for offices that live in PDFs. If that is your day, stay.
