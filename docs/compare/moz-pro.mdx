# Moving from Moz Pro to ZTools Growth Suite

An SEO suite with rank checks, site crawls, the On-Page Grader and AI Overview and prompt tracking on its larger plans. Choose Moz Pro if a team needs rank checks, crawls and AI Overview tracking across many keywords, with scheduled and branded reports.

Choose ZTools if you want the same kinds of checks for a small site without a $49 entry price. Growth Suite runs on a ZTools account with a Growth plan; nothing is imported from Moz Pro, so moving means running your next check here.

**Facts last checked Nov 22, 2026.** Prices change, so check them on <a href="https://moz.com/products/pro" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Moz Pro's site</a>.

## Your Moz Pro jobs, here

| On Moz Pro | In ZTools |
|---|---|
| Crawl and grade a site | [Website Audit](/docs/tools/growth/growth-website-audit) |
| Explore keywords | [Keyword Research](/docs/tools/growth/growth-keyword-research) |
| See AI Overview mentions | [AI Brand Visibility](/docs/tools/growth/growth-brand-visibility) |
| Check page speed | [Page Speed Scan](/docs/tools/growth/growth-page-speed) |

## Switching over

1. Export the reports you want to keep from Moz Pro first. Nothing carries across.
2. Pick a [Growth plan](https://ztools.zaions.com/growth/pricing) and sign in.
3. Run one job you do every week on the matching page above, and compare the result with your last Moz Pro report.
4. Read [where Moz Pro stays ahead](#where-moz-pro-stays-ahead) before you cancel anything.

## Where Moz Pro stays ahead {#where-moz-pro-stays-ahead}

- Rank checks with scheduled and branded reports for a team.
- Tracked prompts on a schedule on its larger plans.
