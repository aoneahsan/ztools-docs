# Moving to ZTools from another tool site

None of these sites hands anything over: no accounts, recipes or stored files carry across. Moving means opening your next job in ZTools, so each guide starts with the page for each job, then says what you would be giving up.

Facts in every guide link to the page we read them on, last checked Nov 22, 2026. The same facts side by side are on [Other tool sites](https://ztools.zaions.com/alternatives).

## PDF work

- [iLovePDF](/docs/compare/ilovepdf): A PDF tool site with web, mobile and desktop apps and a paid Premium plan.
- [Smallpdf](/docs/compare/smallpdf): A PDF tool site from Switzerland with a mobile app, a Chrome extension and Pro, Team and Business plans.
- [PDF24 Tools](/docs/compare/pdf24): A free, ad-supported PDF tool site from a German company, with a Chrome extension and a Windows desktop app that keeps files local.

## File conversion

- [Convertio](/docs/compare/convertio): A file converter for 300+ formats that runs on their servers and sells monthly credit plans.
- [CloudConvert](/docs/compare/cloudconvert): A server-side file conversion service and API sold by conversion credits, with a small free daily allowance.
- [Zamzar](/docs/compare/zamzar): An online file converter with free use capped at two files a day and three monthly plans for bigger files and more volume.

## Developer and data tools

- [CyberChef](/docs/compare/cyberchef): An open-source web app for encoding, encryption, hashing and data operations that chains steps into a recipe, all in the browser.
- [IT Tools](/docs/compare/it-tools): An open-source collection of developer utilities you can use online or self-host with Docker.

## Several kinds of job

- [TinyWow](/docs/compare/tinywow): A free PDF, image, video, file and AI writing tool site with no sign-up and paid Unlimited plans per category.
- [123apps](/docs/compare/123apps): A set of 51 free web apps for video, audio and PDF with one Premium subscription that removes ads.
- [Online Tools (Browserling)](/docs/compare/online-tools): A large collection of image, text and data utilities from Browserling that run in your browser, with a limited free tier and paid passes.
- [10015 Tools](/docs/compare/10015-tools): A free all-in-one toolbox for text, image, PDF, social media, coding, CSS and color jobs, with Chrome and Firefox extensions.

## SEO and AI-search suites

These guides compare a suite with ZTools Growth Suite, the part of ZTools that audits a site, suggests keywords, asks AI models about your brand and drafts articles. Growth Suite needs a Growth plan, and most of these suites go deeper than it does; each guide says where.

### SEO suites

- [Semrush](/docs/compare/semrush): A large SEO and marketing platform with keyword, competitor, audit and AI search visibility tools, sold in tiers per website count.
- [Ahrefs](/docs/compare/ahrefs): An SEO platform with Site Explorer, Keywords Explorer, Site Audit, Rank Tracker and Brand Radar for AI answers.
- [SE Ranking](/docs/compare/se-ranking): An SEO platform for in-house teams and agencies with rank tracking, competitor and backlink research, audits and an AI visibility add-on.
- [Moz Pro](/docs/compare/moz-pro): An SEO suite with rank checks, site crawls, the On-Page Grader and AI Overview and prompt tracking on its larger plans.
- [Mangools](/docs/compare/mangools): A set of five SEO tools (KWFinder, SERPChecker, SERPWatcher, LinkMiner, SiteProfiler) with an AI Search Watcher.
- [Seobility](/docs/compare/seobility): An SEO tool for audits, rank monitoring, backlinks and uptime checks, priced in euros, with AI visibility tracking in beta.
- [Ubersuggest](/docs/compare/ubersuggest): Neil Patel's keyword and SEO tool, sold as a one-time lifetime price with an optional AI add-on.

### AI search and content

- [Surfer](/docs/compare/surfer): A content optimization tool whose Content Editor and AI Tracker aim to get pages cited in AI answers and Google.
- [Frase](/docs/compare/frase): An SEO and GEO content platform that researches, writes and audits articles and publishes them to your CMS.
- [Otterly.AI](/docs/compare/otterly-ai): A monitor for how your brand shows up in ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Copilot and other AI search.
- [Peec AI](/docs/compare/peec-ai): An AI search analytics platform for marketing teams that tracks prompts, sources and competitors across AI models.
