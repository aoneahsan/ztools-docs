# Moving from Otterly.AI to ZTools Growth Suite

A monitor for how your brand shows up in ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Copilot and other AI search. Choose Otterly.AI if AI-answer visibility is the number you report on and you want it tracked daily, with recommendations.

Choose ZTools if asking AI models about your brand when you need to is enough, and you also want an audit and keyword ideas in the same plan. Growth Suite runs on a ZTools account with a Growth plan; nothing is imported from Otterly.AI, so moving means running your next check here.

**Facts last checked Nov 22, 2026.** Prices change, so check them on <a href="https://otterly.ai" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Otterly.AI's site</a>.

## Your Otterly.AI jobs, here

| On Otterly.AI | In ZTools |

| See which AI crawlers visit | [AI Visibility Tracking](/docs/tools/growth/growth-visibility-tracking) |

## Switching over

1. Export the reports you want to keep from Otterly.AI first. Nothing carries across.
2. Pick a [Growth plan](https://ztools.zaions.com/growth/pricing) and sign in.
3. Run one job you do every week on the matching page above, and compare the result with your last Otterly.AI report.
4. Read [where Otterly.AI stays ahead](#where-otterly-ai-stays-ahead) before you cancel anything.

## Where Otterly.AI stays ahead {#where-otterly-ai-stays-ahead}

- Daily prompt tracking across several AI engines, with recommendations.
- An API, MCP access and a Data Studio connector on its larger plans.
