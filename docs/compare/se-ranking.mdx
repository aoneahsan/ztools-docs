# Moving from SE Ranking to ZTools Growth Suite

An SEO platform for in-house teams and agencies with rank tracking, competitor and backlink research, audits and an AI visibility add-on. Choose SE Ranking if you run SEO for several clients and want white-label reports, rank tracking and an API in one place.

Choose ZTools if you look after one or a few sites and want audits, keywords and AI-answer checks for less than a tenth of the price. Growth Suite runs on a ZTools account with a Growth plan; nothing is imported from SE Ranking, so moving means running your next check here.

**Facts last checked Nov 22, 2026.** Prices change, so check them on <a href="https://seranking.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">SE Ranking's site</a>.

## Switching over

1. Export the reports you want to keep from SE Ranking first. Nothing carries across.
2. Pick a [Growth plan](https://ztools.zaions.com/growth/pricing) and sign in.
3. Run one job you do every week on the matching page above, and compare the result with your last SE Ranking report.
4. Read [where SE Ranking stays ahead](#where-se-ranking-stays-ahead) before you cancel anything.

## Where SE Ranking stays ahead {#where-se-ranking-stays-ahead}

- White-label reports and client seats for agencies.
- Daily AI visibility tracking on several engines; Growth Suite checks when you ask.
