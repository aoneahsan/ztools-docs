# Moving from Ubersuggest to ZTools Growth Suite

Neil Patel's keyword and SEO tool, sold as a one-time lifetime price with an optional AI add-on. Choose Ubersuggest if you would rather pay once than every month, and keyword ideas plus a weekly audit cover what you need.

Choose ZTools if you want a monthly plan you can stop, with page speed checks, an AI brand check and analytics chat beside the keyword ideas. Growth Suite runs on a ZTools account with a Growth plan; nothing is imported from Ubersuggest, so moving means running your next check here.

**Facts last checked Nov 22, 2026.** Prices change, so check them on <a href="https://ubersuggest.com/en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Ubersuggest's site</a>.

## Switching over

1. Export the reports you want to keep from Ubersuggest first. Nothing carries across.
2. Pick a [Growth plan](https://ztools.zaions.com/growth/pricing) and sign in.
3. Run one job you do every week on the matching page above, and compare the result with your last Ubersuggest report.
4. Read [where Ubersuggest stays ahead](#where-ubersuggest-stays-ahead) before you cancel anything.

## Where Ubersuggest stays ahead {#where-ubersuggest-stays-ahead}

- A one-time lifetime price instead of a monthly plan.
- Keyword research with ChatGPT and Gemini built in.
