# Moving from Convertio to ZTools

Convertio is a file converter for 300+ formats that runs on its servers and sells monthly credit plans. If you convert large or unusual formats in bulk and want server capacity you can buy by the credit, it is the stronger choice. ZTools does not try to match that format list.

ZTools has converters for the common formats beside the rest of its tools, most running in your browser with no upload and no credit to spend.

**Facts last checked Nov 7, 2026.** The formats and credit prices come from <a href="https://convertio.co" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Convertio's site</a> and its pricing page.

## Format by format

|---|---|

| Many images at once | [Batch image converter](/docs/tools/image/image-format-batch-converter) |

| Audio | [Audio Format Converter](/docs/tools/media/audio-format-converter) |
| Word to PDF | [Word to PDF](/docs/tools/pdf/word-to-pdf) |
| CSV and JSON | [CSV and JSON](/docs/tools/conversion/csv-json) |

## Checking your formats

1. Note the from and to formats you actually use on Convertio.
2. Find each in the table above or among the [conversion tools](/docs/tools/conversion).
3. Convert one real file and open the result in the program it is meant for.
4. Missing a format here? Keep Convertio for that one job.

## Convertio's ground

- Unusual formats. Convertio states far more of them.
- Very large files. A browser tool works within your device; Convertio plans buy 1 GB to 10 GB on its servers.
