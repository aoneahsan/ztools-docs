# Moving from Frase to ZTools Growth Suite

An SEO and GEO content platform that researches, writes and audits articles and publishes them to your CMS. Choose Frase if you publish many articles and want research, writing and publishing to WordPress or Webflow in one flow.

Choose ZTools if you need a full site audit and keyword research as well as articles, on one Growth plan. Growth Suite runs on a ZTools account with a Growth plan; nothing is imported from Frase, so moving means running your next check here.

**Facts last checked Nov 22, 2026.** Prices change, so check them on <a href="https://www.frase.io" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Frase's site</a>.

## Switching over

1. Export the reports you want to keep from Frase first. Nothing carries across.
2. Pick a [Growth plan](https://ztools.zaions.com/growth/pricing) and sign in.
3. Run one job you do every week on the matching page above, and compare the result with your last Frase report.
4. Read [where Frase stays ahead](#where-frase-stays-ahead) before you cancel anything.

## Where Frase stays ahead {#where-frase-stays-ahead}

- Publishing straight to WordPress, Webflow, Sanity or Wix.
- A research-to-publish flow built for teams that ship many articles.
