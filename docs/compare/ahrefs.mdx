# Moving from Ahrefs to ZTools Growth Suite

An SEO platform with Site Explorer, Keywords Explorer, Site Audit, Rank Tracker and Brand Radar for AI answers. Choose Ahrefs if competitor and backlink research are the core of your work and you will use Site Explorer and Keywords Explorer every day.

Choose ZTools if you need the everyday SEO checks and an AI-answer check for a few sites, without paying for a backlink index. Growth Suite runs on a ZTools account with a Growth plan; nothing is imported from Ahrefs, so moving means running your next check here.

**Facts last checked Nov 22, 2026.** Prices change, so check them on <a href="https://ahrefs.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Ahrefs' site</a>.

|---|---|

| Look up keywords | [Keyword Research](/docs/tools/growth/growth-keyword-research) |

## Switching over

1. Export the reports you want to keep from Ahrefs first. Nothing carries across.
2. Pick a [Growth plan](https://ztools.zaions.com/growth/pricing) and sign in.
3. Run one job you do every week on the matching page above, and compare the result with your last Ahrefs report.
4. Read [where Ahrefs stays ahead](#where-ahrefs-stays-ahead) before you cancel anything.

## Where Ahrefs stays ahead {#where-ahrefs-stays-ahead}

- Site Explorer and backlink research. Growth Suite has no backlink index.
- Rank Tracker and SERP history over time.
