# Moving from Semrush to ZTools Growth Suite

A large SEO and marketing platform with keyword, competitor, audit and AI search visibility tools, sold in tiers per website count. Choose Semrush if SEO is a big part of your job and you want competitor research, position tracking and site audits across several websites.

Choose ZTools if you want an audit, keyword ideas, AI-answer checks and an article writer from $9 a month, next to hundreds of everyday tools. Growth Suite runs on a ZTools account with a Growth plan; nothing is imported from Semrush, so moving means running your next check here.

**Facts last checked Nov 22, 2026.** Prices change, so check them on <a href="https://www.semrush.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Semrush's site</a>.

## Switching over

1. Export the reports you want to keep from Semrush first. Nothing carries across.
2. Pick a [Growth plan](https://ztools.zaions.com/growth/pricing) and sign in.
3. Run one job you do every week on the matching page above, and compare the result with your last Semrush report.
4. Read [where Semrush stays ahead](#where-semrush-stays-ahead) before you cancel anything.

## Where Semrush stays ahead {#where-semrush-stays-ahead}

- Position tracking and competitor research across several websites, which Growth Suite does not do.
- Keyword data with search volumes; Growth Suite works from Google Suggest.
