# Moving from Zamzar to ZTools

Zamzar is an online file converter. Free use is capped at two files a day, and three monthly plans buy bigger files, more volume and online storage. If you convert many large files and want them kept online for later, those plans are made for you.

If two a day is not enough, ZTools has no such daily count on its converters, and the rest of its tools sit in the same place at no cost. Most run in your browser, which also means there is no stored copy to come back to.

**Facts last checked Nov 7, 2026.** We read them on <a href="https://www.zamzar.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Zamzar's site</a>, on its sign-up page.

## Before you cancel

1. Download everything stored in your Zamzar account. Nothing moves across.
2. Convert one real file on the matching page above and open it in the program you use.
3. Save results to your own device, since ZTools converters do not keep them for you.

## Zamzar still wins on

- Online storage of converted files, up to 100 GB on its Business plan.
- Very large files. Its Business plan states 2 GB per file.

## What Zamzar's pages say

They gave no tool count and said nothing about accounts, ads, an Android app or a browser extension, so those rows are left out. On the ZTools side: the count is on the [tool index](/docs/tools); most tools need no sign-up, though Growth Suite and Speak do; there are no third-party ad networks, only promotions for our own products on the free plan; and there is an [Android app](/docs/guides/android-app) and an [extension](/docs/guides/browser-extension).
