# Moving from Zamzar to ZTools

Zamzar states 2 free files a day and stores your files on its servers on paid plans. ZTools has no such daily count on its converters, and most run in your browser, so there is no stored copy to come back to. It also means no online storage.

**Facts last checked Nov 7, 2026.** Read them on <a href="https://www.zamzar.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Zamzar's own site</a>, since prices move.

## Which page does which job

| On Zamzar | In ZTools |
|---|---|
| Images | [Image Converter](/docs/tools/image/universal-image-converter) |
| HEIC to JPG | [HEIC to JPG](/docs/tools/conversion/heic-to-jpg) |
| Audio | [Audio Format Converter](/docs/tools/media/audio-format-converter) |
| Word to PDF | [Word to PDF](/docs/tools/pdf/word-to-pdf) |
| PDF to Word | [PDF to Word](/docs/tools/pdf/pdf-to-word) |
| PDF to images | [PDF to Image](/docs/tools/pdf/pdf-to-image) |

## Steps

1. Download any files stored in your Zamzar account. Nothing moves across.
2. Convert one real file on the matching page above and open the result.
3. Save results to your own device, since ZTools converters do not keep them for you.

## What stays with Zamzar

- Online storage of converted files, up to 100 GB on its Business plan.
- Very large files. Its Business plan states 2 GB per file.
