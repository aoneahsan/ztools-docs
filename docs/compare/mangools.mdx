# Moving from Mangools to ZTools Growth Suite

A set of five SEO tools (KWFinder, SERPChecker, SERPWatcher, LinkMiner, SiteProfiler) with an AI Search Watcher. Choose Mangools if keyword research is the main job and you want KWFinder, SERP checks and rank tracking in one calm set of tools.

Choose ZTools if you also want a site audit, page speed checks and an article writer on the same account. Growth Suite runs on a ZTools account with a Growth plan; nothing is imported from Mangools, so moving means running your next check here.

**Facts last checked Nov 22, 2026.** Prices change, so check them on <a href="https://mangools.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Mangools' site</a>.

## Your Mangools jobs, here

| On Mangools | In ZTools |
|---|---|
| Find keywords | [Keyword Research](/docs/tools/growth/growth-keyword-research) |
| Watch AI search answers | [AI Brand Visibility](/docs/tools/growth/growth-brand-visibility) |

## Switching over

1. Export the reports you want to keep from Mangools first. Nothing carries across.
2. Pick a [Growth plan](https://ztools.zaions.com/growth/pricing) and sign in.
3. Run one job you do every week on the matching page above, and compare the result with your last Mangools report.
4. Read [where Mangools stays ahead](#where-mangools-stays-ahead) before you cancel anything.

## Where Mangools stays ahead {#where-mangools-stays-ahead}

- KWFinder, SERPChecker and SERPWatcher built around keyword and ranking work.
- Rank tracking over time, which Growth Suite does not offer.
