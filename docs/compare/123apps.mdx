# Moving from 123apps to ZTools

123apps is a set of 51 free web apps for video, audio and PDF, with one Premium subscription that removes the ads. If you mostly cut, convert or edit video and audio and one low subscription suits you, it fits well, and for long video work it is the stronger tool.

ZTools covers those basics and adds text, code, image, SEO and PDF tools, with no third-party ads on the free plan.

**Facts last checked Nov 7, 2026.** The figures come from <a href="https://123apps.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">123apps</a> and its pricing page.

## Media jobs and where they live here

| On 123apps | In ZTools |

| Join audio | [Audio Joiner](/docs/tools/media/audio-joiner) |
| Convert audio | [Audio Format Converter](/docs/tools/media/audio-format-converter) |
| Compress a video | [Video Compressor](/docs/tools/media/video-compressor) |
| Video to GIF | [Video to GIF](/docs/tools/media/video-to-gif) |
| Merge PDFs | [Merge PDF](/docs/tools/pdf/merge-pdf) |

## Trying your media jobs here

1. Run your usual job on a short clip before a long one.
2. Audio Format Converter and Video Compressor work in your browser by default and offer a server mode. On a slow device, try the server mode.
3. Look around the [media tools](/docs/tools/media) for what sits next to them.
4. Paying for 123apps Premium? Keep it until your real files come out right here.

## What 123apps does better

- Long video editing. 123apps states a 10 GB file limit and is built around that work.

## 123apps, as its pricing page puts it

The pricing page did not say whether files stay in your browser, whether you need an account, or whether there is an Android app or a browser extension, so those rows are left out. ZTools answers each on its own pages: most tools run in your browser, with [AI Background Remover](/docs/tools/image/background-remover), [Image to Text](/docs/tools/media/image-to-text-ocr) and [PDF OCR](/docs/tools/pdf/pdf-ocr-text-extraction) running only on our server; most need no sign-up, though Growth Suite and Speak do; and the [Android app](/docs/guides/android-app) is on Google Play, the [extension](/docs/guides/browser-extension) on the Chrome Web Store.
