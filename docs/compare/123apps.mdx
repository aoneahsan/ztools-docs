# Moving from 123apps to ZTools

123apps states 51 apps, weighted toward video and audio. ZTools has those basics plus text, code, image and SEO tools, without third-party ads on the free plan. For heavy media editing, compare carefully first.

**Facts last checked Nov 7, 2026.** Read them on <a href="https://123apps.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">123apps's own site</a>, since prices move.

## Which page does which job

| On 123apps | In ZTools |
|---|---|
| Cut audio | [Audio Cutter](/docs/tools/media/audio-cutter) |
| Join audio | [Audio Joiner](/docs/tools/media/audio-joiner) |
| Convert audio | [Audio Format Converter](/docs/tools/media/audio-format-converter) |
| Compress a video | [Video Compressor](/docs/tools/media/video-compressor) |
| Video to GIF | [Video to GIF](/docs/tools/media/video-to-gif) |
| Merge PDFs | [Merge PDF](/docs/tools/pdf/merge-pdf) |

## Steps

1. Try your usual media job on a short clip first.
2. Audio Format Converter and Video Compressor run in your browser by default and offer a server mode. On a slow device, the server mode is the one to try.
3. Open the [media tools](/docs/tools/media) to see what else sits next to them.
4. If you pay for 123apps Premium, keep it until your real files have come out right here.

## What stays with 123apps

- Long video editing. 123apps states a 10 GB file limit and is built around that work.
