# Moving from Seobility to ZTools Growth Suite

An SEO tool for audits, rank monitoring, backlinks and uptime checks, priced in euros, with AI visibility tracking in beta. Choose Seobility if you want regular automatic crawls of a larger site, uptime monitoring and prices in euros.

Choose ZTools if a monthly audit, keyword ideas and AI-answer checks are enough and you do not need automatic crawls or uptime checks. Growth Suite runs on a ZTools account with a Growth plan; nothing is imported from Seobility, so moving means running your next check here.

**Facts last checked Nov 22, 2026.** Prices change, so check them on <a href="https://www.seobility.net/en/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Seobility's site</a>.

## Switching over

1. Export the reports you want to keep from Seobility first. Nothing carries across.
2. Pick a [Growth plan](https://ztools.zaions.com/growth/pricing) and sign in.
3. Run one job you do every week on the matching page above, and compare the result with your last Seobility report.
4. Read [where Seobility stays ahead](#where-seobility-stays-ahead) before you cancel anything.

## Where Seobility stays ahead {#where-seobility-stays-ahead}

- Automatic crawls on a set interval, and uptime monitoring.
- Rank monitoring and backlink analysis.
