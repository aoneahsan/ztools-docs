# Moving from Peec AI to ZTools Growth Suite

An AI search analytics platform for marketing teams that tracks prompts, sources and competitors across AI models. Choose Peec AI if a marketing team needs shared AI-search dashboards with sources and competitors, for many users.

Choose ZTools if one person needs to ask AI models about a brand now and then, along with audits and keyword ideas, for $9 a month. Growth Suite runs on a ZTools account with a Growth plan; nothing is imported from Peec AI, so moving means running your next check here.

**Facts last checked Nov 22, 2026.** Prices change, so check them on <a href="https://peec.ai" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Peec AI's site</a>.

## Switching over

1. Export the reports you want to keep from Peec AI first. Nothing carries across.
2. Pick a [Growth plan](https://ztools.zaions.com/growth/pricing) and sign in.
3. Run one job you do every week on the matching page above, and compare the result with your last Peec AI report.
4. Read [where Peec AI stays ahead](#where-peec-ai-stays-ahead) before you cancel anything.

## Where Peec AI stays ahead {#where-peec-ai-stays-ahead}

- Shared AI-search dashboards for many users, with sources and competitors.
- Daily tracking across the AI models you choose.
