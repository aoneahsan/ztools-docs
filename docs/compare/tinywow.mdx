# Moving from TinyWow to ZTools

TinyWow is a free site for PDF, image, video, file and AI writing jobs, with no sign-up and paid Unlimited plans per category. If you want AI writing tools next to PDF and video tools, and you are fine with files going to their server, it does that well.

The thing to check first is where your file goes. TinyWow says files are processed on its side and deleted after an hour. Most ZTools tools stay in your browser, and there is an Android app and a browser extension too.

**Facts last checked Nov 7, 2026.** Plans and limits are from <a href="https://tinywow.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">TinyWow's site</a>, its support and premium pages.

## Trying it with your usual jobs

1. Name the three TinyWow tools you open most.
2. Find each in the [tool index](/docs/tools), or search from the [ZTools home page](https://ztools.zaions.com).
3. Read the processing line on each tool page. Video Compressor and Video to GIF offer a server mode; use it only if your device struggles.
4. AI writing tools send your text to a provider. Read the [privacy page](/docs/privacy) before pasting anything private.

## Where TinyWow is the better pick

- AI writing. TinyWow states 50+ AI writing tools; if those are your main job, compare them first.
- Nothing to close. No account is needed there, or for most tools here.
