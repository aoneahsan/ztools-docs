# Moving from Surfer to ZTools Growth Suite

A content optimization tool whose Content Editor and AI Tracker aim to get pages cited in AI answers and Google. Choose Surfer if writing and refreshing long articles is your job and you want live guidance while you write.

Choose ZTools if you want an audit and keyword research beside the writer, on one Growth plan. Growth Suite runs on a ZTools account with a Growth plan; nothing is imported from Surfer, so moving means running your next check here.

**Facts last checked Nov 22, 2026.** Prices change, so check them on <a href="https://surferseo.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Surfer's site</a>.

## Your Surfer jobs, here

| On Surfer | In ZTools |
|---|---|
| Write an article with AI | [AI Article Writer](/docs/tools/growth/growth-article-writer) |
| Audit existing content | [Website Audit](/docs/tools/growth/growth-website-audit) |
| Research keywords | [Keyword Research](/docs/tools/growth/growth-keyword-research) |
| Check how AI answers mention you | [AI Brand Visibility](/docs/tools/growth/growth-brand-visibility) |

## Switching over

1. Export the reports you want to keep from Surfer first. Nothing carries across.
2. Pick a [Growth plan](https://ztools.zaions.com/growth/pricing) and sign in.
3. Run one job you do every week on the matching page above, and compare the result with your last Surfer report.
4. Read [where Surfer stays ahead](#where-surfer-stays-ahead) before you cancel anything.

## Where Surfer stays ahead {#where-surfer-stays-ahead}

- A Content Editor with live guidance while you write.
- Brand tracking across ChatGPT, Gemini and AI Overviews on a schedule.
