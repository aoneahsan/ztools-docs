# What's new in ZTools

Useful tools. One workbench. This release brings the Workbench design to the web app, an app view on phones,
guided tours, and plans you can buy.

<!-- truncate -->

## Guided tours and a walkthrough

- On the home page, Speak, Meeting notes, Plans, the dashboard and account settings, the tour button shows a short tour
  of that page. The first time you visit one of these pages, a hint says: "Take a quick tour of this page".
- [How ZTools works](https://ztools.zaions.com/walkthrough): six short screens show what ZTools does. Start anywhere.

## An app view on phones

On a phone, home, the dashboard, account settings, sign-in and the first-run screens open as app screens, with a bottom
bar for Home, Dashboard, Speak, Plans and Account. Web and Android · Many tools work without signing in.

## Plans

ZTools offers a free plan and paid plans. Each paid plan's price, billing period and allowances are shown on the
[Plans page](https://ztools.zaions.com/growth/pricing) before you pay. Payments are processed by Polar. ZTools never
receives your card number.

## Shared AI

If Anthropic's shared credit is unavailable, ZTools sends the same request from its server to OpenAI instead, within the
same allowance. If neither can answer, AI pauses.
